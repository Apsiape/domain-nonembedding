\section{Lean formalization}\label{app:lean}

The nonembedding clauses of Theorems~\ref{thm:main} and~\ref{thm:shear}
are proved in Lean~4 with Mathlib. For every prime \(p\), admissible
marked-line data exist. For every admissible datum \(d\), every field
\(K\) of characteristic \(p\), and every division ring \(D\), no unital
ring homomorphism from either \(K[d.\mathrm{Gamma}]\) or
\(K[d.\mathrm{Shear}]\) to \(D\) is injective. No external mathematical
hypothesis is used. The following table gives the principal
correspondences. Every name has prefix \texttt{DomainNonembedding.};
\texttt{M}, \texttt{P} and \texttt{S} stand for \texttt{Main},
\texttt{Permutation} and \texttt{ShearNonembedding}, respectively.

\paragraph{Data and groups.}
The structure \texttt{PaperData}, together with its predicate
\texttt{Admissible}, records the finite field and numbering, distinct
marks, nonzero directions, punctured incidences, girth, density and
exception bounds, and exactly two blocks with bijective coordinates at
each regular point. These are the conditions of
Section~\ref{sec:lines}; in particular, the paper's deterministic datum
is admissible. The independent blind statement read returned
\emph{FAITHFUL}. The all-data theorems therefore cover that datum.
The witness selected by choice in \texttt{paperData} is not identified
with the paper's numbered presentation.

For the paper's datum, \texttt{d.BaseGroup} is the fundamental group of
the split complex of Section~\ref{sec:geometry}, with triangle
relations~\eqref{eq:triangle-relation}; its normalized line words are
exactly~\eqref{eq:free-generators}. The Lean presentation collapses an
explicit spanning tree: row and column zero on one distinguished line,
and one edge \(L_{b,0,0}\) at each apex. The deterministic presentation
in Section~\ref{sec:finish} instead chooses the first spanning tree.
These are presentations of the same based complex; changing the tree
and base paths identifies the groups and transports the normalized
line bases by a common conjugation.

With these identifications, \texttt{d.Gamma} is precisely the
permutation graph group \(\Gamma_p\) of Section~\ref{sec:hyperbolic},
with edge maps~\eqref{eq:hyp-edge-maps} and
relations~\eqref{eq:hyp-stable-letters}: all pairs \((\pi,i)\) are
retained, one stable letter conjugates the whole line basis, and only
\(t_{\pi,i_*}\) is killed. The separate cones \(Z_i\) in that section
prove concentration; they impose no further relation on \(\Gamma_p\).
Likewise, \texttt{d.Shear} is the shear group \(\Lambda_p\) of
Section~\ref{sec:finish}, given by~\eqref{eq:final-presentation} over
the same base. The abbreviation \texttt{Lambda} uses the selected
datum. Thus the all-data results give the nonembedding clauses for the
paper's groups, without equating that selected datum with the paper's.
These presentation identifications are the comparison with the text;
no Lean theorem identifying the two numbered choices is asserted.

\paragraph{Rank and scope.}
The imported primitive \texttt{NonSofic.rankD} is left row rank, from
the unchanged FiniteLean rank module. The paper uses right column rank.
The bridge is proved as \nolinkurl{DivisionRingRank.row_eq_column} and
\nolinkurl{rowRank_eq_columnRank}, with the prefix above; right column
rank is encoded by transpose over the opposite division ring. Both the
primitive and bridge are in the axiom audit, and the bridge is an
export root. Interpolation descends through the regular representation
over the prime field; the target need not contain the extension field.
For shear, subtracting a conjugated matrix gives a half-rank lower
bound, sufficient against~\eqref{eq:final-budget}. This does not prove
the full-rank central-eigenvalue argument of
Lemma~\ref{lem:rank-forcing} or Theorem~\ref{thm:principle} in full.

Hyperbolicity, diffuseness, concentration, the domain property, finite
presentability, torsion-freeness, the shear retraction and its kernel
structure, bi-orderability, left-orderability and characteristic-zero
claims are not formalized. In the shear proof a map to an auxiliary
HNN construction proves the added generators \(s_i\) nonidentity;
no injection or isomorphism of the whole shear group into that
auxiliary group is claimed.

\paragraph{Recorded checks.}
The default build passed with \(3657\) jobs. The full axiom audit
covered \(2792\) declarations, including private, generated and reused
declarations, plus three release wrappers; only \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound} occur. The development
and default target contain no \texttt{sorry}; the Mathlib-only challenge
has three intentional template holes outside that target. All eleven
frozen proposition bodies and both helper types were preserved in the
authorized release form.

The sandboxed \texttt{lake comparator} check against that challenge
passed, with networking disabled and no supplied exports, for data
existence and both all-data, all-field nonembedding statements. A
proof-closure export with sixteen roots, including the row/column
bridge and general shear bound, passed \texttt{leanchecker} and
\texttt{nanoda}; the latter allowed only the three axioms above.
The negative control removing \texttt{Classical.choice} was rejected.
Release lint and a fresh-clone cache retrieval, build and full audit
also passed, with zero admissions.

The shipped folder differs from the checked package only in comment
text, documentation and the removal of internal working files. It was
checked again from a clean copy: cache retrieval, the default build
(\(3657\) jobs), the full audit, the release-target audit, the sandboxed
comparator and the release lint passed, with no \texttt{sorry} outside
the challenge. The declaration list and every axiom line are identical
to the earlier audit, and the re-exported proof closure has the same
SHA-256, so the kernel replays and the negative control apply to it
unchanged.
